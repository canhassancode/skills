# Lint (deprecated)

Retired rather than superseded. `lint` swept the Obsidian vault for conformance to `CONVENTIONS.md` — Class A leaks, frontmatter, naming, archive markers, index reconciliation, overview bloat. Once the vault stopped being agent-authored, its remaining files became hand-written and hand-written files do not generate hygiene debt at this volume. The one sweep worth running was a one-off, not a standing skill.

The rules still live in `~/Obsidian/CONVENTIONS.md`, and the write-skills still enforce them forward. Nothing enforces them retroactively, by decision.
