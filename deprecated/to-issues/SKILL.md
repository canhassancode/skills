# to-issues (deprecated)

Superseded by [`to-tickets`](../../engineering/to-tickets/SKILL.md). "Issue" was biased toward GitHub/Linear; "ticket" is tracker-neutral. `to-tickets` breaks a spec into tracer-bullet vertical slices, each declaring its **blocking edges**, and works either against a real tracker (native blocking links) or a local `tickets.md` file. It publishes `ready-for-agent` by construction — no `/triage` step on the planning lane.

Run `/to-tickets` instead.
