# Wayfinder (deprecated)

Superseded by [`align`](../../engineering/align/SKILL.md). Wayfinder charted an oversized, foggy effort as a map of investigation tickets on the tracker. Its fog is now a pass's Unresolved list, each entry carrying a **Route** — research, prototype, task, pass or decide — and a route that outlives the pass becomes a child of the alignment ticket. The decision is recorded in [ADR-0010](../../docs/adr/0010-align-owns-the-routes-and-wayfinder-retires.md).
